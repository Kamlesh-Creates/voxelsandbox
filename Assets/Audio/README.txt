Place custom sound effects in these folders if desired:
- Footsteps/ (Grass, Dirt, Stone, Sand, Wood footsteps)
- Blocks/ (Block break and place sounds)
- Animals/ (Cow moo, Sheep baa, Pig oink)
- Zombies/ (Zombie groan)
- Ambient/ (Wind, day/night ambience)

If left empty, AudioManager automatically uses procedural audio synthesis.
